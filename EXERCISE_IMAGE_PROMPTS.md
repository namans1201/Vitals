# Exercise Image Prompts

**Reference style (applies to every prompt below):** Each illustration is a stylized anatomical fitness-illustration — a grayscale/desaturated human figure rendered with semi-transparent skin so the underlying muscle structure is visible underneath, with exactly ONE muscle highlighted in solid red/orange so it visually pops against the rest of the body. Each image is a two-panel side-by-side sequence: the starting position on the left, the peak/contracted position of the movement on the right. Real gym equipment relevant to the exercise is rendered realistically in the scene. The background is plain white/neutral, and the camera holds a clean three-quarter or side angle that clearly shows the joint action — professional and anatomically informative, matching the visual language of commercial exercise-database sites (e.g. Muscle & Motion, Fitness Volt), not cartoonish. Individual prompts below restate these constants briefly so each one works standalone if copy-pasted alone into an image tool.

**File naming / slug convention:** Every exercise name below maps to a slug — lowercase the name, replace any run of characters that isn't `a-z`/`0-9` with a single hyphen, then trim leading/trailing hyphens (e.g. "Single-arm DB row" → `single-arm-db-row`, "DB Arnold press" → `db-arnold-press`). Once generated, the static image for an exercise is expected at `public/exercises/<slug>.png` and the animated version at `public/exercises/<slug>.gif` — slugs must stay identical everywhere (this file, the GIF prompts file, and the app).

# Mid-back (rows)

## Inverted row
**Muscle group:** Mid-back (rows) (equipment: bodyweight — sturdy low bar or table edge)
**How to do it:** Lie on your back under a sturdy table or low bar, gripping it slightly wider than shoulder width, body held straight from head to heels. Pull your chest up to the bar by driving your elbows down and back, then lower under control. Bend your knees to make it easier, or elevate your feet on a chair to make it harder.
**Image prompt:** A stylized anatomical fitness illustration: a grayscale, semi-transparent-skin human figure with underlying muscle structure visible, on a plain white background, shown as a two-panel side-by-side sequence. Left panel: the figure hangs under a sturdy waist-high bar, body straight as a plank, arms fully extended, chest below the bar. Right panel: the same figure with chest pulled up to touch the bar, elbows bent and driven back past the torso, body still rigid and straight. The mid-back muscles (rhomboids/mid-trapezius) are highlighted in solid red/orange, the only area of color on the otherwise desaturated body. Camera: clean three-quarter side angle clearly showing the elbow-pulling action and the bar.

## Single-arm DB row
**Muscle group:** Mid-back (rows) (equipment: dumbbell) — unilateral
**How to do it:** Kneel with one hand and one knee planted on a bench or chair, torso flat and parallel to the ground, holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand hanging straight down. Pull the dumbbell up to your hip by driving your elbow back, squeeze for a second, then lower with control.
**Image prompt:** A stylized anatomical fitness illustration: a grayscale, semi-transparent-skin figure with visible muscle structure, plain white background, two-panel side-by-side sequence. Left panel: the figure kneels with one hand and knee braced on a bench, flat back, the working arm hanging straight down holding a dumbbell. Right panel: the same pose with the dumbbell pulled up to the hip, elbow driven back past the torso. Only one arm is working the row — the bracing arm and both knees/legs stay static and resting throughout. The mid-back muscle on the working side is highlighted in solid red/orange against the otherwise grayscale body. Camera: three-quarter angle showing the bench, the dumbbell, and the elbow path clearly.

# Lats (pull-ups)

**Muscle group:** Lats (pull-ups) (equipment: pull-up bar)
